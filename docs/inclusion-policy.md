# Inclusion policy

An athlete may appear in the public tracker only when a primary club, league, federation, or governing-body source confirms at least one of the following:

1. Israeli citizenship or nationality; or
2. official representation of Israel in senior or youth international competition.

Jewish heritage alone is not sufficient. Dual nationals are eligible when the source establishes the Israeli connection.

## Publication requirements

A public record also needs:

- a stable registry identity and provider identifier;
- a current club/competition source;
- a retrieval timestamp and HTTPS source URL;
- sourced statistics, or an explicit `identity-only` state with no numbers;
- a verified photo URL and usage note, or the neutral photo fallback.

Records awaiting evidence use `pending` status. Pending records are excluded from public totals, filters, rankings, and map locations.

Provider errors never trigger fabricated replacement data. A previous verified value may remain visible as `stale`; otherwise the refresh fails closed.
